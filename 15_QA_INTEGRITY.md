# 15 QA and Integrity Checklist

## Collection
- all sources attempted/logged
- raw captures preserved
- quotas/rate limits logged
- query IDs preserved
- secrets not exposed

## Data
- no fabricated records
- provenance complete
- duplicates measured
- spam rules documented
- private content excluded
- personal identifiers hashed/removed

## NLP
- validation sample exists
- sentiment evaluated
- topics inspected
- sarcasm/ambiguity handled
- Indonesian edge cases inspected

## Statistics
- denominators shown
- sensitivity analyses run
- persona stability checked
- dominant threads checked

## Business interpretation
- social signal not presented as population truth
- purchase intent only where evidence supports it
- academic/expert/customer evidence kept separate
- hypotheses labeled
- unsupported uniqueness claims removed

## Visuals
- n and denominator visible
- timeframe visible
- exclusions visible
- no misleading axes

## Final
- every major number reproducible
- every major chart generated from saved analysis data
- limitations disclosed
- proposal recommendations linked to evidence
