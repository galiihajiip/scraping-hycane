## Qualitative patterns
- **Indonesian price talk is about building and starting, not buying kits.** It covers component and nutrient prices (most often quoted between roughly Rp10 ribu and Rp100 ribu per item), total setup or start-up capital ("modal") quoted around Rp2–8 juta for small business installations, and whether to make AB mix yourself because the commercial one feels expensive ("kemahalan").
- **English price talk** compares integrated smart gardens (AeroGarden, Gardyn, Click & Grow) with DIY buckets and totes, and criticises proprietary pods (~$5 each).
- **"Worth it" questions** cluster around smart-garden models and grow lights, which is purchase exploration.

## Implications for the Rp910,000 HYCANE kit
- The main competitor is **DIY**, not other kits. Price must be justified by avoided failures, time saved, and bundled consumables.
- An **entry tier** (media + guidance + basic system, sensors optional) reduces the barrier for the frugal majority.
- Consumables must be **open and transparently priced** to avoid pod-lock-in resentment.
