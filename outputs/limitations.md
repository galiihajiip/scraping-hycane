# Limitations

1. **Not a population survey.** The corpus is public, self-selected online discourse. Counts describe what was said in accessible sources, not how many people in Indonesia (or anywhere) hold a view. No market size, prevalence, or willingness-to-pay figure can be derived from it.
2. **Indonesian coverage comes almost entirely from YouTube.** 30,703 Indonesian customer-voice records (33.7%) were obtained, but 99% come from comments on videos found with Indonesian-first queries, so their share reflects query design. Other Indonesian arenas (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook groups, WhatsApp communities, Tokopedia/Shopee reviews, Kaskus, Kompasiana) were not accessible: no API access, private groups, ToS prohibitions or robots restrictions. Many Indonesian comments are short appreciation or questions, which lowers per-record pain rates.
3. **Platform skew.** YouTube provides 60% of customer voice and Reddit 21%. English-language platforms (Reddit, Hacker News, Lemmy, Mastodon, GardenWeb) skew toward tech-literate hobbyists in North America and Europe. Dominant-platform-excluded sensitivity analyses are reported, but they cannot add missing populations.
4. **Reddit via a third-party archive.** Arctic Shift data can lag deletions/edits on Reddit and scores are point-in-time. The official API would be preferred.
5. **Sampling decisions.** Hydroponic subreddits were sampled up to 100 posts per calendar quarter (later quarters are capped, earlier ones complete); comment threads were sampled round-robin by subreddit × year (300 threads after a mid-run cap); YouTube comments were capped at 300 top-level threads per video; GardenWeb threads were a seeded-random subset of listing links, with at most 50 answers per thread; r/DWC was dropped mid-run. These caps reduce viral-thread dominance but mean counts are not proportional to platform activity.
6. **Cannabis context.** English hydroponic discourse includes substantial cannabis growing. Text-based flagging removes explicit mentions only; implicit cannabis threads (e.g., photos with "should I harvest?") remain in the core set and may inflate nutrient/light topics.
7. **Rule-based labels have modest accuracy.** Pain points, intents, experience, features, price, sustainability and contradiction probes are transparent regex rules. On the held-out half of the reference sample, pain-point detection reached micro-precision 0.27 and recall 0.31 (any-pain precision 0.48), and intent reached accuracy 0.45 and macro-F1 0.29 (`outputs/tables/rule_validation.json`). Absolute counts are therefore indicative; the relative pain ranking proved stable across sensitivity subsets. The rules were revised once using the dev half of the sample, and the test half was not inspected.
8. **Validation is LLM-referenced, not human-verified.** Reference labels were produced by an LLM reading each sampled record. Human annotation (`data/annotation/annotation_queue_human.csv`) is still required before claiming human-validated accuracy.
9. **Sentiment model fit.** The production model was trained on tweets; long technical posts are often labelled AMBIGUOUS or NEUTRAL. Sentiment is used descriptively, never as purchase intent.
10. **Personas are discourse segments.** Most authors appear once; clusters describe kinds of posts, not people. No demographic attribute (age, gender, income) is observable or inferred; the age 20–40 hypothesis cannot be tested with this data.
11. **Geography.** Location is known only from explicit community context or explicit self-reports, so almost all records have no country.
12. **Academic evidence is abstract-level.** Curated findings paraphrase abstracts; full texts were not read. OpenAlex "journal" status does not guarantee rigorous peer review. Semantic Scholar was rate-limited.
13. **Bagasse.** Public discourse barely mentions bagasse as a growing medium, and the academic index contains no water-culture hydroponic test of bagasse media. Neither customer desirability nor technical performance of bagasse media is established by this study.
14. **Time.** Records span many years; older discussions may describe products and prices that no longer exist. Recent-versus-persistent comparisons are in the timeline tables.
