# 22 Final Insight Framework

The final analytical chain must be:

**Signal → Evidence → Pattern → Pain/Need → Customer Type → Desired Outcome → Product Response → Business Value → Validation Test**

## Insight quality levels

### Level 1: Interesting
Single or isolated signal. Do not change strategy from this alone.

### Level 2: Recurring
Repeated within a platform or topic. Worth monitoring.

### Level 3: Cross-platform
Repeated across multiple independent source families. Candidate strategic insight.

### Level 4: Triangulated
Customer signal aligns with academic/expert evidence. Strong strategic basis.

### Level 5: Validated
Triangulated and supported by primary validation or product experiment. Strongest basis for proposal claims.

## Insight card template

- Insight ID
- One-line finding
- Evidence level
- Customer corpus n
- Platforms
- Timeframe
- Sentiment
- Related topics
- Pain point
- Persona
- Desired outcome
- HYCANE feature implication
- Revenue/business implication
- Contradicting evidence
- Confidence
- Primary validation needed
- Source IDs

## The five most important final questions

1. What is the strongest customer pain?
2. What is the strongest evidence that customers want a solution?
3. What is the strongest evidence that challenges HYCANE?
4. Which HYCANE features are truly evidence-backed?
5. What must the team validate next before making a hard business claim?

## Decision rule

A finding should influence the proposal strongly only when it is either cross-platform recurring, triangulated, or validated.

A beautiful chart with weak evidence is not a strong finding.
