# 14 Final Report Structure

1. Executive Summary: collection period, raw n, gold n, source families, major findings, top pains, intents, feature signals, personas, implications.
2. Research Context: advisor request and HYCANE hypothesis.
3. Research Questions.
4. Data Sources and Coverage.
5. Methodology: discovery, collection, cleaning, dedup, spam, language, sentiment, topics, intent, personas.
6. Data Quality.
7. Sentiment Findings.
8. Topic Findings.
9. Pain-Point Findings.
10. Customer Intent.
11. Feature Demand.
12. Personas.
13. Academic/Expert Triangulation.
14. Existing HYCANE Hypothesis Validation.
15. Contradictions.
16. HYCANE Strategic Recommendations.
17. Proposal-Ready Insights.
18. Limitations.
19. Next Validation Plan.
20. Reproducibility.

Every major conclusion must link to a saved table or evidence record.
