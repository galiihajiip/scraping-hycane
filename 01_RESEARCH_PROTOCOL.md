# 01 Research Protocol

## Design
Exploratory computational social-listening study with three distinct evidence streams:

**A. Customer Voice:** public posts/comments/reviews for pains, sentiment, intent, desired outcomes, feature requests, complaints, and buying signals.

**B. Academic/Technical:** peer-reviewed and institutional evidence for technical plausibility, known barriers, and context.

**C. Expert/Industry:** credible grower, agronomist, educator, researcher, practitioner commentary for interpretation.

Never merge these streams into one sentiment denominator.

## Research questions
At minimum answer: how people discuss hydroponics; sentiment; recurring pains; interest/aspiration; barriers; problem-solving behavior; purchase signals; feature demand; sustainability; bagasse relevance; beginner/expert differences; platform differences; Indonesia/global differences; contradictions; evidence strength.

## Sampling
Use multi-stage discovery: broad seed queries -> platform discovery -> pain-point expansion -> feature/purchase expansion -> competitor/product discovery -> saturation check.

## Corpus target
Minimum 5,000 unique public comment/post records. Preferred 10,000+, stretch 20,000+. If impossible because of legitimate access constraints, maximize permitted data and document the gap. Do not fabricate.

## Balance
Track platform, source family, language, geography, date, source type, query cluster, topic cluster. Add thread-level caps or weighting so one viral object does not dominate.

## Time
Use the widest practical public timeframe. Report earliest/latest observation and collection date. Compare recent vs persistent themes.

## Geography
Priority Indonesia, then Southeast Asia, then global English discourse, then additional languages where quality permits. Infer geography only from explicit metadata or source context.

## Interpretation
Use phrases such as "within the observed public corpus" and "public digital discourse suggests". Do not present social listening as a representative national survey.
