# 12 Visualization and Dashboard Specification

Create an analytical, clean dashboard. Avoid decorative AI-style graphics.

## Required charts
1. Platform coverage.
2. Source-family coverage.
3. Timeline.
4. Sentiment by platform.
5. Sentiment by topic.
6. Top pain points.
7. Pain-point frequency x severity.
8. Intent distribution.
9. Intent by platform.
10. Feature demand.
11. Persona distribution.
12. Persona x pain points.
13. Persona x features.
14. Language distribution.
15. Geography/country where defensible.
16. Platform x topic heatmap.
17. Evidence-strength distribution.
18. HYCANE pain-to-feature mapping.
19. Optional co-occurrence network.
20. Optional intent funnel.

## Every chart must show
Title, n, denominator when meaningful, timeframe, source/analysis layer, and material exclusions.

## Dashboard filters
Platform, date, language, country, experience, intent, sentiment, topic, persona.

## Static exports
PNG and SVG where appropriate. Use deterministic filenames.

## Proposal-ready subset
Produce a concise subset for the business proposal: top pain points; social-listening evidence matrix; evidence-backed persona; feature mapping.
