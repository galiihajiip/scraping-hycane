# 18 Decisions and Assumptions

1. Social listening is exploratory market insight, not a population survey.
2. Minimum target: 5,000 unique real public comment/post records.
3. Preferred target: 10,000+, stretch 20,000+.
4. Customer voice, academic evidence, and expert evidence remain separate.
5. Existing HYCANE segmentation is a hypothesis to validate.
6. Indonesia is the commercial priority, while global discourse is used for comparison/benchmarking.
7. Public-data collection must respect access controls, ToS, rate limits, robots, and privacy.
8. Blocked platforms become documented coverage gaps, not targets for bypass.
9. Sentiment alone is insufficient.
10. Personas must emerge from evidence.
11. Feature demand must be evidenced, not assumed from the product concept.
12. Numeric willingness-to-pay requires explicit price evidence or primary research.
13. Sustainability value must be tested, not assumed.
14. Major findings must survive sensitivity analysis where possible.
15. Viral source dominance must be controlled or disclosed.

Maintain an assumption register:
`ID | Assumption | Reason | Evidence | Confidence | Validation plan`.
